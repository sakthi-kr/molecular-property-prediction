# Frozen test results

This report summarises the **one-time held-out evaluation** of the previously selected models on MoleculeNet Lipophilicity (experimental logD at pH 7.4). It is based on the locally generated `final_report.json` from the frozen experiment. This repository intentionally does not contain compound-level labels, predictions, trained models, or the source dataset.

## Evaluation design

- 4,200 validated molecular records; 15 2D descriptors and 2,048-bit Morgan fingerprints (radius 2, with chirality).
- Independent, fixed random-grouped and scaffold-held-out 80/10/10 partitions: 3,360 training, 420 validation, 420 test records in each strategy.
- All canonical-molecule groups remain in one partition in both strategies; all Bemis–Murcko scaffold groups remain in one partition under the scaffold strategy.
- Four baseline models were compared on validation data. The primary **Ridge + Morgan** model uses `alpha=10` and was frozen before test evaluation.
- Experimental logD is **not** logP, target affinity, ADMET suitability, toxicity, or clinical efficacy.

## Held-out test metrics

| Model | Random test RMSE | Scaffold test RMSE | Scaffold test MAE | Scaffold test R² |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Dummy mean | 1.179 | 1.211 | 1.001 | −0.011 |
| Ridge — descriptors | 0.999 | 1.036 | 0.842 | 0.260 |
| Random Forest — descriptors | 0.828 | 0.928 | 0.726 | 0.406 |
| **Ridge — Morgan fingerprints** | **0.792** | **0.923** | **0.733** | **0.412** |

All errors are in logD units. Metrics are rounded from the locked local report; unrounded scaffold Ridge RMSE was **0.923454**. Relative to the dummy baseline, the scaffold RMSE reduction is about **23.7%**. The Ridge and Forest test RMSE difference is only about **0.004**, and does not demonstrate meaningful superiority.

## Prediction intervals and applicability

- A **separate calibration Ridge model** generated nominal 90% intervals; do not treat these as intervals centred on the full-training Ridge prediction.
- On the 420 scaffold-test compounds, the empirical interval coverage was **372/420 = 88.6%**, with a mean full interval width of **3.02 logD units**.
- Molecules with weak Morgan/Tanimoto similarity to training molecules had larger errors. Errors and regression-to-the-mean effects were notable at the low/high ends of logD.
- Scaffold separation is more demanding than a random split, but does not guarantee a truly novel chemical series or prospective generalisation. Similarity bands in the demo are descriptive **heuristics**, not calibrated confidence categories.

## Interpretation and reuse

These are retrospective experimental results for one published benchmark. One 420-compound scaffold test split cannot establish drug-discovery usefulness, accuracy on new assays or formulations, clinical validity, or regulatory compliance. We did not tune models using held-out test scores. No dataset rows or generated predictions are redistributed here. For provenance, original records, fixed split contracts and checksums, see the local preparation/training/analysis/final reports generated by the pipeline and the [reproducibility guide](reproducibility.md).
